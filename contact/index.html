What to have ready when you call

A few details let dispatch assign the right plumber and let you start limiting damage while you wait.

  • The street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening, in plain words, such as water coming through a ceiling or a toilet that will not stop running.
  • Whether you have found and closed the main water shutoff.
  • Whether anyone in the house smells gas. If so, leave first and call the gas utility or 911.
  • A phone number where the plumber can reach you on the way.
