Vintonville Historic District · Westborough, MA

Plumber in Vintonville, Westborough, MA

Vintonville is a small residential neighborhood east of Westborough's town center. It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2006, and its 80 properties sit on Cottage, Elm, Spruce, Green, Pine, Brigham, Cedar, South and Beach Streets.

If a drain is slow, a faucet drips or the water heater is misbehaving, call (508) 389-4045, give the street and describe the problem, and ask what service is available for your address.

Vintonville at a glance

Where it is

East of the town center, about 20 acres, with properties on nine named streets.

What is there

80 properties, described as modestly sized houses on smaller lots, built mainly between 1860 and 1890.

Recognition

Added to the National Register of Historic Places on August 23, 2006, reference number 06000717.

Mailing address

Westborough, MA. Cottage Street addresses are listed under ZIP code 01581.

What a house from 1860 to 1890 can and cannot tell you

The listing describes the district's houses as modestly sized and built on smaller lots, mainly between 1860 and 1890. That tells you the buildings are old. It does not tell you what is inside the walls.

A house that age has usually been updated by several owners over many decades, so the pipes in yours may be new, old or a mix of both. We do not guess from the year. A plumber who looks first and tells you what was found will give you a better answer than one who assumes.

Two quick checks help before any visit. Find the main shutoff and make sure it turns. Then note where the water meter is and whether the water heater sits in a basement, a closet or an addition.

Questions worth asking when the house is old

Bring these to the plumber, whoever you hire.

  • Which pipes can be seen in the basement or crawl space, and what are they made of?
  • Has the plumber looked inside the wall at the problem fixture, or only at the fixture itself?
  • Would a camera look into the drain make sense before a big repair? A sewer camera inspection shows the pipe from the inside.
  • What does the repair option cost compared with replacement, and what does each one include?
  • Does the job need a permit from the Westborough Building Department?

Historic listing and your plumbing

National Register listing recognizes the neighborhood's history. Whether any local review applies to changes on your property is a question for the Westborough Building Department at Town Hall, 34 West Main Street, and for the town's historical commission.

Plumbing repairs themselves fall under the state plumbing code, 248 CMR 10.00. Permit rules vary by job, so ask the Building Department before the work starts.

Before you call about a plumbing problem

Four steps make the call faster and protect the house.

Stop the water if it is running

Close the fixture shutoff or the main valve. In an older house, turn valves gently and never force one that is stuck.

Keep drains quiet

If a drain is backing up, stop running water and flushing until a plumber has looked at it.

Note what you see

Write down where the problem is, when it started and what changed. A photo helps.
