The municipal wells: PFAS, iron and manganese in the news

Hopkinton Independent reported in May 2025 that PFAS was found in Well 6 in 2023 and that a filtration system followed. Town officials had said in 2022 that the supply was affected by PFAS, iron and manganese.

The EPA set a federal limit of 10 parts per trillion for six PFAS in April 2024, and MassDEP's guideline had been 20 parts per trillion. We do not make health claims. If you have questions about a specific tap, a certified lab can test it.

What a plumber does with this: iron and manganese leave stains and sediment that clog aerators and shower heads, so faucet repair and heater flushing come up more often in towns with those minerals.

The private-well home

If your house is on a private well, the town does not treat your water. The system is your own: a well pump, a pressure tank, a pressure switch and whatever treatment equipment was added.

The tank holds a cushion of air so the pump does not cycle every time a tap opens. When the air charge fails the pump short-cycles and wears out. Pressure that swings between high and low, or a pump that runs every few seconds, is the textbook sign.

Signs your well system needs attention

These are the symptoms private-well owners report most.

  • The pump cycles on and off every few seconds when a tap runs.
  • Pressure rises and falls rapidly at a shower.
  • Water spits air from a tap after the pump restarts.
  • Brown or orange staining appears at fixtures, or a metallic taste is noticeable.
  • The pressure tank is waterlogged and feels heavy when you tap it.
  • The electric bill rises and the pump housing feels hot.

Drought, the 2025 outdoor ban and irrigation leaks

In 2025 the town banned outdoor water use during a drought, and officials described wells under strain. Water rates rose 40 percent for fiscal 2025, which makes a hidden leak more expensive than it used to be.

A buried irrigation line can leak into the yard without showing a puddle. Shut the sprinklers off, read the meter and read it again after 15 minutes. If it moves, a leak detection visit finds the line.

Treatment equipment and the water heater

Iron and manganese, whether from a well or a municipal main, settle in the bottom of a tank water heater. Sediment makes a tank rumble, cuts capacity and shortens its life. Draining the tank each year helps, and on a gas unit the sediment layer acts as an insulator over the burner.

When a tank fails, water heater replacement is the route, and a sediment filter ahead of the heater is a sensible add-on. We install equipment, and a lab confirms what is in the water.

I-495 divides Hopkinton into east and west zones, and I-90 crosses the north. Route 135 and Route 85 are the other main roads. Whitehall State Park lies on Route 135 and Hopkinton State Park is in the north, both landmarks for directions.

Hopkinton village is the center and Woodville, in the west, became a historic district in 2005. Tell dispatch if you are east or west of the highway, and give the nearest main road. The Dell EMC headquarters site is also a useful reference for the commercial side.

Ashland lies nearby, and our Grafton page covers the southwest side of the region for crews that cross over.

A town with a reservoir history

Boston took Lake Whitehall for its water supply in 1894, and the reservoir was later removed from service, becoming a state park in 1947. The Hopkinton Reservoir inside Hopkinton State Park was also taken out of service as a Boston source.

Those reservoirs no longer feed city taps, so they do not affect household plumbing today. The point for a homeowner is that lakeside land often has a high water table, and a sump pump matters more near low ground.

If a pipe or pump fails in Hopkinton

These four steps work for town-water and well-water homes.

Close the main or kill the pump

On town water, close the main valve. On a well, turn the pump breaker off so it does not keep pushing water into a break.

Cut power near the wet area

Switch off breakers for any room with standing water and keep clear of the pump pit if it is flooded.

Clear the area

Move electronics and boxes off the floor and open cabinet doors under affected sinks.
