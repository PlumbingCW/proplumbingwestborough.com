After-hours calls: what to do before the plumber arrives

Commercial water damage compounds fast because it reaches inventory, electrical panels and neighbors in the same building. The first ten minutes decide how far it spreads.

Find the building's main water shutoff and close it. If a tenant space has its own valve, use that to keep the rest of the building running. Then call us with the address, the suite and what you can see.

  • Close the main or suite shutoff valve and note where it is for the next person.
  • Move stock, paper records and electronics off the floor.
  • Shut off power to any panel or outlet that water has reached.
  • Photograph the leak, the ceiling and the equipment for the insurer.
  • Tell the property manager and leave the building access instructions with dispatch.

What commercial plumbing involves

A commercial building is not a large house. Pipe runs are longer, fixtures are in groups, and floor drains, restrooms, break rooms and mechanical rooms each have their own failure points.

We handle repairs, drain clearing, fixture replacement, water heater service, water line work and leak tracing. We also coordinate with other trades when a repair involves roofing, electrical or fire protection.

If the job is a full renovation or a tenant build-out, we scope it as a project after the emergency is stable, and that work follows our plumbing installation process.

Leak detection in an occupied building

Commercial leak tracing is a single, honest service for us. We use moisture meters, thermal imaging and listening equipment to find a leak behind finishes, then open the smallest section needed.

We are candid that finding a leak in a building with several tenants or shared risers can take more than one visit. Where a leak crosses suites, we report what we found so the property manager and the insurer can decide who pays.

Grease traps, floor drains and the kitchen line

Food service businesses depend on a grease interceptor to keep fats out of the sewer. When the interceptor is full, the grease backs up into the kitchen drains and the floor drain.

Pumping a grease trap is typically a scheduled maintenance task, while clearing a congealed line is an emergency. We handle the line, and we help you set a service interval so the trap does not overflow on a Saturday night.

Backflow prevention and testing

A backflow preventer keeps dirty water from flowing back into the public supply. Many commercial properties with irrigation, fire lines or boilers have one, and these devices are usually tested on a schedule.

Test requirements and schedules are set locally and by the water supplier, so ask the Westborough Water Department and the Building Department what applies to your property. We can test and repair devices when the requirement is confirmed.

Have this ready when you call dispatch

A clear first call saves ten minutes on site. Gather these details if you can do it safely.

  • The street address, the suite or unit, and the entrance to use after dark.
  • Where the main shutoff and any suite valves are, and whether they are closed.
  • What you see: spray, drip, standing water, sewage or no water at all.
  • Who can authorize the repair and who to bill.
  • Whether the building has a fire sprinkler or boiler tied to the same supply.

Property managers and tenant calls

If you manage several units, tell us which are covered by which owner so repair authorization is clear at the time of the call. We can bill the management company, the owner or the tenant as you direct.
